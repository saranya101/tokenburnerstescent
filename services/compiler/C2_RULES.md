# Phase C2 rule evaluation

C2 accepts grounded IDs and numeric state from Contract V1.1. It never accepts language or LLM output. C3 can pass candidate operations and a projected state to `evaluate_constraints`, then check `PolicyEngine` separately before constructing a plan. C2 does not create or simulate routes.

Customer constraints answer whether approved request requirements and enabled persistent protections are met. Each merged effective rule retains its winning provenance. Minimum balances merge by maximum; cost, lock-in, and transaction maxima merge by minimum; excluded accounts union. Disabled hard rules are ignored. A hard rule for a different user fails closed. Persistent V1 currently supports `MIN_AVAILABLE_BALANCE`, `EXCLUDED_ACCOUNT`, and `MAX_SINGLE_TRANSACTION`; it has no persistent `MAX_LOCK_IN_DAYS` variant.

An account-specific minimum checks that active account's available minor units. A currency-wide minimum sums `availableMinorUnits` across all active accounts in that currency. Frozen and closed account balances do not count. This is liquidity, not ledger balance; it makes no transferability or settlement assumption. A missing account contributes zero. Equality passes.

Cost accounting uses integer minor units. Transfers, bill payments, and purchases count external principal/price plus explicit fees. Internal moves count fees only. An FX conversion counts its source principal once only when a later external debit is provably funded by that conversion; its source-currency fee always counts. Sale fees count, but sale proceeds do not subtract from total spending. FX fees come from the snapshot quote. For a maximum in the FX source currency, attribution requires a valid unexpired candidate quote, one FX credit into an initially empty active destination account, and downstream external debits from that same account within the rounded FX credit. The source principal is counted once when first used; the downstream target principal and same-currency fee are then omitted to avoid double-counting. Other foreign spending or fees, mixed account funding, unsupported FX directions, and unavailable quotes fail closed with structured errors. C2 does not invent exchange rates. `MAX_SINGLE_TRANSACTION` checks the nominal amount/price of each operation in its rule currency, excluding fees; an operation in another currency fails closed because C2 has no conversion basis for comparing the transaction to the limit. The C1 `OperationResult.cost` is a gross debit, whereas C2 classifies financial cost separately.

For `MAX_LOCK_IN_DAYS`, a trusted caller may attach lock-in days to any operation. Current non-buy actions create no lock-in product and default to zero. A buy without supplied duration fails closed because the V1 asset has no lock-in field. This metadata is internal and does not change the wire contract.

Bank policy checks service availability, statuses, capabilities, asset tradability, and an operation allowlist. C1 reuses the same bank permission facts as local action preconditions and also checks amounts, balances, quotes, and holdings. Policy does not apply effects. Its structured decisions can later map to `POLICY_BLOCKED` without adding planning here.

## Deferred C3 trade execution boundary

C1 may simulate `BUY_ASSET` and `SELL_ASSET` with a trusted, explicit internal total price and fee. C3 Phase 1 must not synthesize executable trade plans. The team still needs to decide how an approved plan binds price/quote identity and expiry, buy maximum spend protection, and sell minimum proceeds protection. An internal price fixture alone does not bind Person A's execution.
